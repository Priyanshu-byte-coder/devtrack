# DevTrack sponsor brief

DevTrack turns GitHub activity into a game: a score, tiers, XP, a global leaderboard with seasons, duels and player cards for any GitHub user. It is free for every developer, MIT licensed, and built in the open by a large contributor community.

Live: [devtrack.live](https://devtrack.live) · Code: [github.com/Priyanshu-byte-coder/devtrack](https://github.com/Priyanshu-byte-coder/devtrack)

---

## Why sponsor DevTrack

- **A daily habit, not a one-off visit.** Players come back to check their rank, streak and season standing, and share their cards and badges.
- **A contributor magnet.** Hundreds of developers, many making their first open-source contribution, read this repository and its README.
- **A technical audience.** Players and contributors work with Next.js, TypeScript, React, Supabase and the GitHub API.
